Are ATLAS Online programs UGC-recognized?

YourDegree is an online platform that helps students discover, compare, and apply to online degree programs offered by recognized universities across India.

How does YourDegree help students choose the right course?

Tell us your goals, budget and schedule. A certified counsellor compares programs on UGC entitlement, NAAC grade, fees and specialisations, then shortlists the options that fit you.

Is YourDegree connected with recognized universities?

Yes. We only list UGC-entitled programs from recognised universities, and every listing shows its accreditation so you can verify it yourself.

Does YourDegree charge students for counselling?

No. Counselling is completely free. You pay programme fees directly to the university, never to YourDegree.

Are EMI options available for online degree programs ?

Many universities offer no-cost or low-cost EMI plans. Use the Fee & EMI Calculator, or ask your counsellor for the options on a specific program.
